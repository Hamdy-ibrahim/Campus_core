CampusCore is a campus management and engagement web application designed to help students stay connected with everything happening within their university. The application provides separate user and admin dashboards. Students can create an account and access important campus information, including clubs, events, marketplace listings, and announcements, allowing them to easily keep up with school activities and opportunities. Administrators have access to a dedicated dashboard where they can create and manage campus events, announcements, clubs, and marketplace content, as well as view and manage registered users. Overall, CampusCore provides a centralized platform that improves communication, student engagement, and access to campus activities and information.
